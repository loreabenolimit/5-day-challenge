Most women spend years dealing with irritation, itching, or discomfort without ever connecting it to what they are wearing every day.

Conventional panty liners contain some or all of the following:

Chlorine-bleached pulp.

Used to make the product look white and clean. The process can leave trace residues on the material itself.

Synthetic fragrances.

Added to mask odors. Fragrance formulations are considered proprietary, which means brands do not have to disclose what is actually in them. Many contain compounds known to irritate sensitive skin.

Plastic backing and SAP gel.

The waterproof layer and the absorbent core in most disposables are plastic or petroleum-based materials. They trap heat, reduce airflow, and sit against the skin for hours.

Rayon and viscose.

Synthetic fibers often blended into the soft top layer. They can absorb moisture unevenly and, depending on how they are processed, may carry residue.

This is not meant to alarm you. It is just information most brands do not put on the front of their packaging.

Organic cotton has one ingredient: cotton. Grown without synthetic pesticides and processed without chlorine or added chemicals. That is the whole story.

You already made the switch. I just wanted you to know why it matters.
