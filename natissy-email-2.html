Something I wish someone had told me when I first made the switch:

Give them at least one or two washes before you judge.

New organic cotton can feel a little stiff straight out of the packaging. It takes a wash or two to fully open up and reach that soft, lived-in feel you are going to love. So if your very first wear is not quite what you expected, wash them once and try again. You will feel the difference.

A few other things that help:

Pair with breathable underwear.

Cotton or bamboo underneath makes the biggest difference. Synthetic fabrics can work against the airflow that makes organic cotton so comfortable. Small change, big impact.

Change when you need to, not on a schedule.

Follow the same intuition you always have. Organic cotton manages freshness really well, but it still needs you to listen to your body. You know best.

Pay attention on your hardest days.
