A few things I wish someone had told me about switching to reusable liners:

Give it two or three washes first.

New organic cotton gets softer with every wash. If your first wear feels slightly different from what you expected, wash them once before judging. They settle into their best softness within a few uses.

Pair with breathable underwear.

Synthetic fabrics underneath can trap heat and reduce the airflow that makes organic cotton so comfortable. Cotton or bamboo underwear works best with natissy.

Change at the same times you would a disposable.

Reusable does not mean once a day. Follow the same routine you have always had: change when you feel you need to. Organic cotton manages freshness well but it still needs regular changes.

The difference shows up on the days you used to notice discomfort most.

Many women notice it most on the days they were most bothered by conventional liners. Give it a few weeks and pay attention to those days specifically.
