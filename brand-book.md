## 1. Brand at a Glance

**natissy** (tagline: "by nature") makes reusable organic cotton panty liners for women who are done with the irritation, plastic waste, and hidden chemicals in conventional period and daily-wear pads.

The brand was born from a simple desire: to create something purely organic, comfortable, and kind to bodies and the environment. Every product is a small act of care, for the person wearing it and for the planet.

**Customer in one sentence:** A woman who has experienced irritation or discomfort from conventional pads and is actively looking for a cleaner, gentler, more sustainable alternative.

## 2. Logo

The natissy logo features a small bird resting among layers of leaves in earthy greens, oranges, and browns, with "natissy" in a rounded green wordmark and "by nature" in warm terracotta beneath it.

**Usage rules:**
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the letter "n" on all sides.
- Minimum display size: 120px wide on screen, 1 inch wide in print.
- Do not stretch or distort the logo.
- Do not recolor the logo or apply drop shadows or outlines.
- The logo is multi-color and reads best on white or very light neutral backgrounds.
- A white version is not available (the multi-color mark cannot be simplified to a single color without a designer). The Day 5 dashboard handles this gracefully with a fallback.

**File:** Place your logo PNG at `assets/logo.png`.

## 4. Typography

**Heading font:** Nunito (Google Fonts)
Rounded, friendly, approachable. Matches the warmth of the logo wordmark. Use Bold (700) for primary headings and SemiBold (600) for subheadings.

**Body font:** Lato (Google Fonts)
Clean, neutral, highly readable. Use Regular (400) for body copy and Bold (700) for emphasis.

**How to load:**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Nunito:wght@600;700;800&family=Lato:wght@400;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

**Specimen:**
- Headings: Nunito Bold: "Feel good every day."
- Body: Lato Regular: "Made from 100% organic cotton, natissy liners are soft, breathable, and free from synthetic chemicals."

## 5. Imagery

Product and lifestyle photography should feel gentle, real, and grounded in nature.

**Direction:**
- Natural light, soft shadows. No harsh studio lighting.
- Props: cotton plants, leaves, linen fabric, wooden surfaces, plants.
- Models look relaxed and comfortable, not posed or clinical.
- Colors in shots should echo the brand palette: soft greens, warm neutrals, creamy whites.
- Avoid plastic, synthetic-looking environments, or overly medical-looking compositions.

**What to avoid:** Bright neon, high-contrast dramatic lighting, overly polished stock imagery.

Product imagery for the Reusable Panty Liners should highlight the softness and texture of the cotton fabric, and ideally show the product in a natural, at-home setting.

## 6. Voice and Tone

**Core adjectives:** warm, empowering, natural, gentle, honest

**We sound like this:**
- "Your body deserves better than chemicals. So we made something it will actually love."
- "Soft on you, kind to the earth. That is what natissy is about."
- "We started natissy because we were tired of the irritation, the waste, and the ingredients we could not pronounce."

**We never say:** "synergy," "disrupt," "game-changing," "next-level." No corporate speak. No fear-based messaging. No shame.

**We do say:** organic, gentle, real, soft, kind, breathable, clean, conscious, free from, made with care.

**Tone shifts by context:**
- Ads and inserts: shorter, punchy, benefit-first.
- Emails: warmer, more personal, like a note from a friend who knows their stuff.
- Product descriptions: honest and specific, focused on how it feels.

## 7. Audience

**Who buys:** Women (primarily 25 to 45) who have experienced irritation, itching, or discomfort from conventional disposable pads. They are eco-conscious, read ingredient labels, and are willing to invest a little more in products that align with their values.

**The problem they have:** Conventional pads contain plastics, synthetic fragrances, and chemical absorbents that cause irritation. They also generate significant landfill waste. Many women do not realize the connection between their discomfort and the materials in their products.

**The transformation natissy delivers:** After switching to natissy, they feel comfortable throughout the day with no irritation or itching. They feel good about their choice, both for their own body and for the environment. It is a small daily act of self-care that also feels responsible.

**What motivates them to buy:** Irritation relief comes first. Eco-friendliness is the second pull. Once they try it, comfort and softness keep them coming back.
