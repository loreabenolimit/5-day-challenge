01 Brand at a Glance
"by nature"

natissy makes reusable organic cotton panty liners for women who are done with the irritation, plastic waste, and hidden chemicals in conventional pads. The brand was born from a simple desire: to create something purely organic, comfortable, and kind to bodies and the environment.

Every natissy product is a small act of care. For the person wearing it, and for the planet.

The Customer

A woman who has experienced irritation or discomfort from conventional pads and is actively looking for a cleaner, gentler, more sustainable alternative. She reads labels, cares about what goes on her body, and wants her everyday choices to reflect her values.

Positioning

Reusable organic cotton panty liners for women who want everyday comfort without chemicals or waste.

02 Logo

The natissy logo features a small bird resting among layered leaves in earthy greens, oranges, and browns, with "natissy" in a rounded green wordmark and "by nature" in warm terracotta beneath it. It is warm, natural, and immediately recognizable.

Place logo file at assets/logo.png

Usage Rules

  • Keep clear space equal to at least the height of the letter "n" on all sides.
  • Minimum size: 120px wide on screen, 1 inch wide in print.
  • Do not stretch, distort, or recolor the logo.
  • Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or effects.
  • The logo reads best on white or very light neutral backgrounds.
  • A white version of the logo requires a designer (multi-color mark cannot be simplified).

05 Imagery

natissy photography should feel gentle, real, and grounded in nature. Every image should reinforce the feeling of comfort, care, and naturalness.

Direction

  • Natural light with soft, warm shadows. No harsh studio or flash lighting.
  • Props that echo nature: cotton plants, green leaves, linen fabric, wooden surfaces, potted plants.
  • Models look relaxed and at ease, not posed or clinical.
  • Colors in the shot should echo the brand palette: soft greens, warm neutrals, creamy whites.
  • Product shots highlight the texture and softness of the cotton fabric.
  • Lifestyle images show a calm, comfortable home environment.

What to Avoid

  • Bright neon or high-contrast dramatic lighting.
  • Overly polished or generic stock imagery.
  • Plastic, synthetic, or clinical-looking environments.
  • Images that feel sterile or medical.

The Feeling to Aim For

Like a quiet Sunday morning. Soft light through a window, a mug of tea, something that feels taken care of. That is the emotional world natissy imagery lives in.

06 Voice and Tone

Core Voice Adjectives

Warm Empowering Natural Gentle Honest

We Sound Like This

"Your body deserves better than chemicals. So we made something it will actually love."
"Soft on you, kind to the earth. That is what natissy is about."
"We started natissy because we were tired of the irritation, the waste, and the ingredients we could not pronounce."

Words We Use

organic gentle soft kind breathable clean conscious free from made with care

Words We Never Use

synergy disrupt game-changing next-level toxic positivity shame-based copy

Tone by Context

  • Product inserts and ads: Shorter, punchy, benefit-first. Lead with comfort or relief.
  • Emails: Warmer and more personal, like a note from a friend who knows their stuff.
  • Product descriptions: Honest and specific, focused on how it feels and what it is made from.

07 Audience

Who Buys

Women, primarily 25 to 45, who have experienced irritation, itching, or discomfort from conventional disposable pads. They are eco-conscious, read ingredient labels, and are willing to invest a little more in products that align with their values.

The Problem They Have

Conventional pads contain plastics, synthetic fragrances, and chemical absorbents that cause irritation for many women. They also generate significant landfill waste. Many customers do not immediately connect their discomfort to the materials in their products until they discover an alternative.

The Transformation natissy Delivers

After switching to natissy, she feels comfortable throughout the day with no irritation or itching. She feels good about her choice, both for her own body and for the environment. It is a small daily act of self-care that also feels responsible.

What Motivates the Purchase

Irritation relief comes first. Eco-friendliness is the second pull. Once she tries it, comfort and softness keep her coming back and referring to friends.
